Privacy Policy

How IntelliDiagram handles information when your organization uses the app in Atlassian Confluence.

Effective: October 9, 2026Last updated: October 9, 2026
In brief: IntelliDiagram is built on Atlassian Forge and has no data store of its own. Your diagram source is saved inside your Confluence pages, diagrams are drawn in your browser, and the app has no permission to send data anywhere outside Atlassian. We do not receive, sell, or analyze your content.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

IntelliDiagram for Confluence (the “App”) is provided by LH Buyer Technology LLC (“LH Buyer Technology,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), 30 N Gould St, Suite 4000, Sheridan, Wyoming, 82801, United States of America. This policy applies to information handled by the App. Atlassian separately controls information processed by Confluence and the Atlassian platform under Atlassian's own privacy policies.

2. Information the app handles

IntelliDiagram handles only what it needs to draw and save a diagram:

  • Diagram and Markdown source: the text a user writes in the macro editor, with its optional title and chosen dialect. This is saved as parameters of the macro, inside the Confluence page itself.
  • Rendered images: when a user chooses Convert to SVG or Convert to PNG, the rendered image is added to that page as an ordinary Confluence attachment.
  • Page content, on request: to place a converted image directly below its diagram, the App reads the current page body and saves it back with the image added. It does this only when a user clicks a Convert button.
  • Space names: the “Create a practice page” feature lists the spaces the current user can see so they can choose where the page goes.

Every one of these requests is made as the signed-in user, with that user's own Confluence permissions. The App cannot read or change anything the user could not read or change themselves.

3. Information the app does not collect

  • The App has no application database. It does not use Forge hosted storage, and we do not operate servers that receive your content.
  • The App does not store Atlassian account identifiers, names, email addresses, or any other user profile information.
  • The App declares no external network permissions. It cannot send your diagram source, page content, or images to us or to any third party.
  • The App uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking technology, and sets no cookies of its own.
  • We do not train machine-learning models on your content and do not sell, rent, or share it.

4. Where diagrams are rendered

Mermaid, Graphviz, and Markdown are rendered by code that runs inside the App's sandboxed frame in the user's own browser. The source is not sent to a rendering service. External images referenced from Markdown are not loaded.

5. Storage, security, and data residency

Because diagram source and converted images are stored as Confluence page content and attachments, they are protected by your Confluence permissions, versioned with page history, and stored in the same location as the rest of your Confluence data. Any data residency settings your organization has applied to Confluence apply to them equally. The App adds no separate storage location.

No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure, but the App is designed so that there is no additional copy of your content to protect. See the Security Policy for the technical controls.

6. When information is shared

We do not receive your content, so we have none to share. Atlassian operates the Forge platform and Confluence on which the App runs. If you contact us for support, we process the information you choose to send us in order to answer you.

7. Retention and deletion

Diagram source lives in your pages for as long as you keep it there. Deleting a macro, an attachment, or a page removes it under Confluence's normal rules, including page history and trash. Uninstalling the App removes its access to your site immediately. Because the App stores nothing of its own, there is no App data to delete after uninstall; diagram source already saved in your pages remains part of those pages.

8. Personal data and data subject requests

The App does not store personal data. If personal information appears inside a diagram or Markdown block, it is part of your Confluence page content and is governed by your organization's Confluence administration. Requests to access, correct, export, or delete it should go to your organization's Atlassian administrator. Support requests you send us can be deleted on request.

9. International processing

The App does not transfer your content outside the Atlassian platform. Atlassian's processing is governed by Atlassian's own terms and data residency controls.

10. Children

IntelliDiagram is a workplace application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as IntelliDiagram or applicable requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page and, when a change is material, note it in the App's Marketplace release notes.
