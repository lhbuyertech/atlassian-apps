Data Processing Addendum

Data protection terms for IntelliDiagram for Atlassian Confluence.

Effective: October 9, 2026Provider: LH Buyer Technology LLC
Purpose. This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) describes how LH Buyer Technology LLC (“Provider”) processes Customer Data on behalf of the organization using IntelliDiagram (“Customer”). It supplements the Terms of Use and applies to the extent Provider processes personal data for Customer through IntelliDiagram.

1. Roles and scope

Customer is the controller of Customer Data, and Provider is the processor. Provider processes Customer Data only to provide, secure, and support IntelliDiagram in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and the Agreement. This DPA does not apply to data Provider processes as an independent controller for its own legal, security, billing, or support obligations.

2. Processing details

Subject matter and purpose

The subject matter is the rendering of text-defined diagrams and Markdown inside Customer's Confluence pages, and, at a user's request, attaching a rendered image to a page.

Nature of the processing

IntelliDiagram has no data store and no ability to transmit data outside the Atlassian platform. Diagram source is stored by Confluence as part of the page; rendering takes place in the user's browser; converted images are stored by Confluence as page attachments. Provider's personnel have no access to Customer Data through the App.

Data categories

  • Diagram and Markdown source, titles, and rendered images that Customer's users create with the App.
  • Confluence page content read and re-saved, on a user's instruction, to place a converted image on that page.
  • Names and keys of Confluence spaces shown to a user choosing where to create a practice page.

The App does not store Atlassian account identifiers or user profile data. Personal data is processed only to the extent Customer's users include it in diagram or page content.

Data subjects

Data subjects may include Customer's employees, contractors, users, collaborators, and other individuals whose information appears in Customer's Confluence content.

3. Provider obligations

Provider will:

  • Process Customer Data only for the purposes described in this DPA, the Agreement, or Customer's documented instructions.
  • Ensure that people authorized to process Customer Data are subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction, as described in the Security Policy.
  • Keep the App free of external data egress and of any data store outside the Atlassian platform, or give notice under section 9 before changing that.
  • Notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data and provide reasonably available information needed for Customer's response.
  • Provide reasonable assistance with data-subject requests, investigations, and regulatory inquiries relating to Provider's processing.

4. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes Provider to use subprocessors necessary to provide IntelliDiagram. The only subprocessor is Atlassian, which provides the Forge runtime, authentication, and access to Confluence APIs under the Forge Data Processing Addendum. IntelliDiagram uses no other subprocessor.

5. International transfers

IntelliDiagram does not transfer Customer Data outside the Atlassian platform. Atlassian's Forge processing is governed by the Forge DPA, which includes applicable Standard Contractual Clauses and related transfer provisions.

6. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for providing lawful instructions, giving required notices, obtaining required permissions, limiting access to authorized users through Confluence permissions, and determining whether particular content is suitable to include in a diagram or page.

7. Return and deletion

Customer Data handled by IntelliDiagram is stored by Confluence as page content and attachments, and is returned, exported, and deleted using Confluence's own features. On uninstall, the App's access ends immediately and there is no separate App data to delete.

8. Audits and information

Provider will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and will cooperate with reasonable Customer audit requests, subject to confidentiality and security. Public security and privacy information is provided through the Security Policy and Privacy Policy.

9. Priority and changes

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Use regarding the processing of personal data, this DPA controls for that conflict. Provider may update this DPA when IntelliDiagram, its subprocessors, or applicable requirements change. Provider will publish the updated version and update the effective date.

10. Contact

For DPA, privacy, or security questions, contact support@lhbuyertech.us or security@lhbuyertech.us.
