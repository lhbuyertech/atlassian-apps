Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays.

Before you contact us

  • Confirm IntelliGlossary is installed on the Confluence site and that the relevant app access is approved.
  • For missing terms, confirm the term is Approved and belongs to the applicable Site-wide or Space glossary.
  • For AI issues, confirm the Definition Assistant is enabled and the configured provider is available.
  • For macro issues, insert the macro from Confluence’s editor rather than pasting copied markup.

Security issues

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to lhbuyertech@gmail.com rather than through a public issue tracker. We aim to acknowledge security reports within one business day.
